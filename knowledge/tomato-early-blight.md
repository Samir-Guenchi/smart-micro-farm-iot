# Early blight (alternaria) on tomatoes and potatoes

**What it looks like:** brown spots with rings like a target, starting on the oldest, lowest leaves, often with a yellow edge. Leaves yellow and drop from the bottom up.

**Why it happens:** a fungus that lives in plant debris and soil. Warm days, dew at night and stressed plants make it worse.

**What to do:**
- Remove the lowest leaves up to the first truss so soil does not splash onto them.
- Mulch the soil.
- Feed plants well: hungry plants get it worse.
- Clear all plant debris at the end of the season.
- Rotate: no tomatoes or potatoes in the same bed for three years.
