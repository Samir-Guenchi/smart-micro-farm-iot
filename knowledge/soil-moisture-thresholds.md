# When to water: reading soil moisture sensors

The irrigation node reports `soil_moisture_pct`, the volumetric water content of the soil around the sensor. The right level depends on the soil, so calibrate first.

**Calibrate once per bed:**
1. Note the reading the day after a heavy soaking, once the water has drained. That is roughly *field capacity* (often 35–45% in loam, 15–25% in sand).
2. Note the reading when the crop first shows wilting at midday and has not recovered by evening. That is your *stress point*.

**Typical starting thresholds for loam** (start watering below, stop around field capacity):

| Crop | Start watering below |
|---|---|
| Lettuce | 30% |
| Tomato, potato | 25% |
| Corn | 25% |
| Broad bean | 22% |

**Tips:**
- Place the sensor in the root zone, 10–15 cm deep for lettuce, 15–25 cm for tomatoes and potatoes, and next to (not under) a dripper.
- A cheap capacitive sensor drifts; re-check field capacity each season.
- Readings rise quickly during watering and keep spreading for an hour; judge by the reading an hour after the pump stops.
- Skip watering if rain over 5 mm is forecast in the next 24 hours and the soil is not yet below the threshold.
