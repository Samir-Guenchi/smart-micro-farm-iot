# Blossom end rot on tomatoes

**What it looks like:** a dark, sunken, leathery patch at the bottom (flower end) of the fruit. It is not a disease and does not spread.

**Why it happens:** the fruit could not get enough calcium, almost always because watering was uneven. When the soil dries out and is then soaked, calcium does not reach the growing fruit. Pots and grow bags suffer most.

**What to do:**
- Keep the soil evenly moist. Drip irrigation on a schedule, or a soil moisture sensor, fixes most cases.
- Mulch with straw to slow drying.
- Remove affected fruit; the next trusses will be fine once watering is steady.
- Adding calcium rarely helps unless a soil test shows the soil is really short of it.
