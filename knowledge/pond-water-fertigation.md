# Irrigating with fish pond water

Fish pond water carries nitrogen and other nutrients from fish waste. Pumping it through drip lines feeds the beds and cleans the pond at the same time.

**How to do it well:**
- Draw water from the middle depth of the pond, not the bottom sludge or the surface scum.
- Filter it: a screen or disc filter of 120 mesh (about 130 microns) before the drip lines, and clean it weekly. Algae clog drippers fast.
- Flush the end of each drip line monthly.
- Top up the pond with fresh water to replace what is pumped out.

**Food safety:** apply pond water to the soil, not onto leaves of crops eaten raw (lettuce). Drip under mulch is ideal. Stop overhead use completely for salad crops.

**Balance:** pond water is rich in nitrogen but low in potassium and phosphorus; fruiting crops (tomatoes) still benefit from compost or wood ash.

**Azolla** grown on the same pond takes up excess nutrients and can be harvested for feed (see the azolla note).
