# Corn: borer, birds and smut

**European corn borer:** small holes in the stem with sawdust-like frass, broken tassels. Cut stalks low after harvest and shred or compost them hot, since larvae overwinter in old stalks.

**Birds and badgers:** take ripening cobs. Bag cobs with paper or mesh bags once the silks brown, or harvest promptly.

**Corn smut:** grey swellings on cobs that burst into black powder. Remove galls before they burst and do not compost them. In Mexico young galls are eaten as *huitlacoche*.

**Poor cobs with gaps:** the plants were not pollinated well. Plant corn in a block of at least 4 × 4 plants, not one long row, and shake the tassels on still mornings.
