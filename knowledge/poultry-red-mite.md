# Red mite in the hen house

**Signs:** pale combs, hens reluctant to go in at night, fewer eggs, tiny red or grey specks in perch cracks and under nest boxes. Mites hide by day and feed at night; check with a torch after dark or wipe a white tissue under the perch.

**What to do:**
- Clean out all bedding and scrub perches and corners with hot water.
- Dust cracks, perches and nest boxes with food-grade diatomaceous earth, and repeat every 5–7 days for a month, since eggs hatch in about a week.
- Use removable perches that you can take out and treat.
- Give hens a dust bath area with dry soil, sand and wood ash.
- Approved acaricides from the vet for heavy infestations; follow egg withdrawal times on the label.
