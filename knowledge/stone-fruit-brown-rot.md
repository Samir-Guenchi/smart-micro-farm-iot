# Brown rot on stone fruit

**What it looks like:** fruit rots on the tree with brown flesh and rings of grey-brown spore pads; blossoms and twigs can wilt and die in spring. Rotten fruit dries into hard 'mummies' that stay on the tree.

**What to do:**
- Remove all mummified fruit from the tree and the ground in winter; they carry next year's infection.
- Prune out dead twigs.
- Thin fruit so they do not touch.
- Control wasps and birds that wound the fruit.
- Pick ripe fruit promptly and handle gently.
