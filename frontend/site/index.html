The loop

Each part of the farm feeds the next. Nothing leaves as waste.

  1. 🐟Fish pond water becomes drip irrigation for vegetables and fruit trees.
  2. 🐔Poultry manure becomes compost for the beds and orchard.
  3. 🥬Trimmings become feed for snails and poultry.
  4. 🌿Azolla from the pond becomes protein feed and green manure.
  5. 🌽Corn grown on site feeds 50 hens and 80 quail.
  6. 🐝10 beehives pollinate every crop and make honey.
