# Peach leaf curl

**What it looks like:** in spring, new leaves pucker, thicken and turn red or purple, then drop. Repeated years weaken the tree.

**Why it happens:** a fungus (*Taphrina deformans*) whose spores sit on the bark and buds and infect as the buds open in cool, wet weather.

**What to do:**
- Spray copper in late winter just before bud burst, and again 2 weeks later; spraying after symptoms appear is too late.
- Keep rain off the tree in spring if it is small or wall-trained (a simple plastic cover from January to May works).
- Pick off and destroy affected leaves; feed and water the tree so it can grow a second set.
- Choose resistant varieties when planting.
