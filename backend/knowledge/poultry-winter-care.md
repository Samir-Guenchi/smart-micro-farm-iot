# Keeping poultry through a cold winter

Hens handle cold well if they are dry and out of draughts; damp and drafts kill, not cold alone.

- **Ventilation without draught:** vents high up under the roof, closed openings at perch level. Condensation on the walls means too little ventilation.
- **Deep litter:** add straw or shavings over the winter up to 20–30 cm; it composts slowly and gives off a little heat.
- **Water:** keep it unfrozen with a heated base or by changing it twice a day.
- **Feed:** a handful of whole corn in the late afternoon helps hens stay warm overnight.
- **Combs:** large combs can get frostbite below about −15 °C; a little petroleum jelly helps.
- **Quail** are less hardy: keep them in a frost-free shed with good light.
- **Coop door:** snow and ice can block an automatic door. Check it closes fully at dusk, and alert if it does not.
