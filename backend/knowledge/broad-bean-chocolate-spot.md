# Chocolate spot on broad beans

**What it looks like:** small chocolate-brown spots on leaves, stems and flowers that can merge into large dark patches in wet weather.

**Why it happens:** a fungus (*Botrytis fabae*) that thrives in damp, crowded plantings, especially autumn-sown beans after a wet winter.

**What to do:**
- Sow at wider spacing (about 20 cm between plants) for airflow.
- Avoid high-nitrogen feeding; potassium helps resistance.
- Do not sow in soil that waterlogs.
- Remove badly affected plants and do not compost them.
- Rotate beans to a new bed each year.
