# Laying hens: daily and weekly care

The farm keeps about 50 hens.

**Daily:**
- Fresh, clean water, always. A hen drinks about 250–500 ml a day, more in heat.
- Layer feed (16–18% protein) available all day, plus a separate dish of crushed oyster shell or limestone for strong eggshells. Corn and trimmings are extras, not the main feed.
- Collect eggs at least once a day, twice in hot weather.
- Check the flock: a hen standing hunched, puffed up, or not eating is sick; separate her.
- Check the coop door opened in the morning and closed at dusk.

**Weekly:**
- Top up dry bedding (straw, wood shavings) and remove wet spots.
- Check for red mite at night (see the red mite note).

**Space:** at least 4 hens per m² inside, 30 cm of perch per hen, one nest box per 4–5 hens, and outdoor run space.

**Light:** hens lay with 14–16 hours of daylight; laying drops in winter. Extra light is optional; rest helps hens live longer.
