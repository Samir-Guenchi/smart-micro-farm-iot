# Black bean aphid on broad beans

**What it looks like:** dense black clusters on the growing tips and young pods, often with ants running among them.

**What to do:**
- Once the first pods have set at the bottom, pinch out the top 8–10 cm of each plant. The aphids gather on the soft tips, and the tips are good to eat steamed.
- Sow in autumn (where winters are mild) or as early as possible in spring, so plants are tough before aphids peak.
- Squash small colonies by hand or wash off with water.
- Ladybirds and hoverflies clear them if you leave flowers nearby and do not spray.
