# Growing azolla as feed and green manure

Azolla is a tiny floating fern that fixes nitrogen from the air and can double in a few days. On this farm it grows on the fish pond.

**Growing:**
- Needs still water, partial shade and 20–30 °C. It struggles above 35 °C and dies off in frost; keep a starter culture indoors over winter in Latvia.
- Harvest a third to half of the mat every few days so it keeps growing and does not cover the whole pond, which would starve the fish of oxygen.

**Feeding:**
- Rich in protein (about 20–25% of dry weight). Hens, quail and fish eat it fresh.
- Replace up to 10–20% of poultry feed with fresh azolla; rinse it first and introduce it gradually.
- Fish (tilapia, carp) eat it directly from the pond.

**Green manure:** dig fresh azolla into beds or add to compost as a nitrogen-rich green.
