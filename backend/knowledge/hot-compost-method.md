# Hot compost from farm waste

A hot pile turns trimmings, manure and straw into compost in about three months and kills most weed seeds and diseases.

**Recipe:** roughly 2–3 parts brown (straw, dry leaves, wood chips, cardboard) to 1 part green (fresh trimmings, grass, kitchen scraps, manure). Chop large material.

**Steps:**
1. Build the whole pile at once, at least 1 m³, layering brown and green.
2. Wet each layer: as damp as a wrung-out sponge.
3. Check temperature with a long thermometer. Aim for 55–65 °C.
4. Turn the pile when the temperature drops, moving the outside to the middle. Three or four turns are usually enough.
5. Cure for 1–2 months before use.

**Troubleshooting:** smells of ammonia, add browns. Does not heat, add greens and water, or make it bigger. Too wet and slimy, add browns and turn.

**Leave out:** diseased plants (blight), weeds in seed if the pile does not get hot, meat, and dog or cat waste.
