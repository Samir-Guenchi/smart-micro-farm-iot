# Lettuce bolting and bitterness in heat

**What it looks like:** the plant stretches upward, the leaves turn bitter and a flower stalk appears.

**Why it happens:** long days and heat (above about 25 °C) tell lettuce to flower. Dry soil makes it worse.

**What to do:**
- In North Africa and southern Europe, grow lettuce in the cool season (September to April).
- In summer, choose heat-tolerant types such as Batavia and romaine, and sow small amounts every 2–3 weeks.
- Give afternoon shade with shade cloth or by planting next to taller crops such as corn or tomatoes.
- Keep the soil evenly moist.
- Lettuce seed may not germinate above 25 °C: sow in the evening, water with cool water, or start seed in a cool room.
- Harvest heads as soon as they firm up.
