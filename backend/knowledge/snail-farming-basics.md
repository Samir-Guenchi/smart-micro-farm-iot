# Snail farming basics

The farm raises snails (heliciculture) for local sale and export.

- **Species:** *Cornu aspersum* (petit-gris and gros-gris) are the most common farmed species in Europe and North Africa.
- **Pens:** outdoor pens with fine mesh fencing and an anti-escape edge (a folded mesh lip or an electric wire). Grow green feed inside (clover, radish, rapeseed) for food and shade.
- **Feed:** fresh greens and vegetable trimmings plus a calcium-rich meal for the shells (crushed limestone or eggshell).
- **Moisture:** snails need damp conditions and are active at night. Mist or drip-irrigate the pens in the evening in dry weather.
- **Cycle:** breeding snails lay eggs in loose soil in spring; young snails reach market size in one season in warm climates. In northern climates they hibernate in winter and are usually harvested in autumn.
- **Hygiene:** clear uneaten food daily to avoid mould and pests.
- Keep pens well away from vegetable beds so escapes do not become a pest.
