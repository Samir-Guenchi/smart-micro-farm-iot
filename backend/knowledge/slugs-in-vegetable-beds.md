# Slugs and snails in vegetable beds

**What it looks like:** irregular holes, chewed seedlings that vanish overnight, slime trails. Worst in wet weather and at night.

**What to do:**
- Water in the morning, not the evening, so the soil surface is dry by night.
- Go out with a torch after dark and pick them off; it works better than anything else in a small plot.
- Put boards or tiles between rows; slugs hide under them by day and can be collected.
- Raise seedlings in trays until they are sturdy, then plant out.
- Ducks are excellent slug hunters if you can fence them out of the crops.
- Ferric phosphate pellets are allowed in organic growing; avoid metaldehyde, which harms pets and wildlife.

**On this farm:** keep the snail farming pens well away from vegetable beds and fenced, so farmed snails do not escape into the crops.
