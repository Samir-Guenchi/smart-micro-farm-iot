# Aphids on lettuce

**What it looks like:** small green, grey or pink insects in the heart of the plant and under leaves; leaves curl and get sticky. Root aphids make plants wilt in dry weather without visible insects on the leaves.

**What to do:**
- Hose small colonies off with a strong jet of water, or rinse leaves in salted water after harvest.
- Encourage ladybirds, hoverflies and lacewings: plant flowers such as phacelia, calendula, dill and alyssum next to beds.
- Avoid too much nitrogen; soft, fast growth attracts aphids.
- Cover young plants with fine mesh.
- Choose varieties resistant to the lettuce aphid (*Nasonovia*) if it keeps coming back.
