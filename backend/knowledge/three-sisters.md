# Three sisters: corn, beans and squash

A traditional planting where each crop helps the others: corn is the pole, climbing beans fix nitrogen and climb the corn, squash covers the ground and shades out weeds.

**How:**
1. When the soil is warm (above 12 °C), make low mounds about 1 m apart. Sow 4–5 corn seeds per mound.
2. When corn is 15 cm tall, sow 4 climbing bean seeds around each mound.
3. A week later, sow squash or pumpkin between mounds, one plant per 2–3 m².

**Tips:**
- Use a strong field corn or tall sweetcorn; a heavy bean variety can pull down short corn.
- Plant as a block so the corn pollinates.
- Drip lines go in before sowing; the squash leaves make access hard later.
- In short northern summers (Latvia, Sweden), start corn and squash in pots and plant out in June.
