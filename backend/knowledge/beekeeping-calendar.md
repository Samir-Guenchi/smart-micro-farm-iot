# Beekeeping calendar

The farm has 10 hives. Months below are for Latvia and Sweden; in Algeria and Morocco everything comes 1–3 months earlier and summer dearth replaces winter as the hard season.

| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| Jan–Feb | Leave hives closed. Heft from behind to check weight; feed fondant if light. Clear snow from entrances. |
| Mar–Apr | First inspection on a warm day (above 14 °C): is the queen laying, are there stores? Clean floors. |
| May–Jun | Swarming season: inspect every 7–9 days, add supers before the brood box is full, make splits (see swarm note). |
| Jun–Aug | Main honey flow. Add supers; harvest capped frames. |
| Aug | Last harvest. Treat for varroa straight after (see varroa note). |
| Sep | Feed sugar syrup (2:1) to build winter stores: about 15–20 kg per hive in the north. Reduce entrances against wasps and robbing. |
| Oct–Dec | Fit mouse guards. Oxalic acid treatment when the colony is broodless (usually December). Leave them alone. |

**The hive scale** on the hive node shows the nectar flow live: a strong colony can gain 2–5 kg a day in a good flow, and a steady weight drop in winter shows how fast stores are used.
