# Citrus pests: scale, aphids and leaf miner

**Scale insects:** small brown or white bumps on stems and leaf veins, sticky honeydew and black sooty mould. Scrub off small infestations with a soft brush and soapy water; summer oil sprays work on larger ones.

**Aphids:** cluster on young shoots in spring. Usually controlled by ladybirds; wash off with water if needed.

**Citrus leaf miner:** silvery winding trails inside young leaves, which curl. Mostly harms young trees. Avoid heavy pruning and nitrogen in late summer, which push the soft flushes it attacks.

**Ants:** they protect scale and aphids from predators. A sticky band around the trunk keeps ants off and lets predators work.

**Potted citrus indoors** in Latvia or Sweden often gets scale and spider mite in winter: check leaf undersides and mist leaves in dry rooms.
