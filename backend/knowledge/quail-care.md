# Quail care

The farm keeps about 80 Japanese quail, mainly for eggs.

**Housing:** quail are ground birds and can fly straight up when scared; a low cage or pen roof (30–40 cm) or a soft netting roof prevents head injuries. Give 0.1–0.2 m² per bird, with sand for dust bathing and shelter from wind and rain.

**Feed:** a game bird or turkey starter (24–28% protein) for chicks, and a layer feed of about 20–22% protein for adults, plus grit and crushed oyster shell. Chicken layer feed alone is too low in protein.

**Water:** shallow drinkers; chicks can drown in deep ones, so add pebbles to the drinker for the first two weeks.

**Laying:** hens start laying at 6–8 weeks and lay almost one egg a day with 14–16 hours of light.

**Ratio:** one male per 4–5 females, or no males at all if you only want eggs.

**Temperature:** comfortable at 18–24 °C. Protect from frost and from heat above 30 °C with shade and ventilation.
